S&T Field Logbook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy explains what data S&T Field Logbook (the “app”) collects, stores and shares. The app is an offline field logbook for railway Signals & Telecommunications (S&T) department staff. It is designed around a simple principle: your data stays on your device and under your control.

1. Data stored on your device

All of the records you create — daily logs, equipment records, materials, timings and your settings (theme, font size, and similar preferences) — are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer or to any server in the normal course of using the app.

2. Google Drive backup (optional)

Drive backup is an optional feature you switch on yourself in the app’s Settings. If you enable it:

  • You sign in with your own Google account.
  • The app reads and writes a backup of your records only in that account’s hidden app-data folder — a private space Google reserves for this app. The app cannot list, read, or modify any of your other Drive files, folders, or documents.
  • The backup exists so your records can be restored if you reinstall the app or move to a new device.

Signing out, or turning Drive backup off in Settings, stops the app from accessing your Google account. Copies already uploaded to your Drive app-data folder remain in your account; you can remove them through your Google account’s app-permissions or app-data settings.

3. What the app does not do

  • No account is required for normal use.
  • No analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party trackers are built into the app.
  • No location tracking.
  • No personal data is collected, sold, or shared with any third party.

4. Exports and sharing

Documents (Word, Excel, PDF) are generated on your device. If you choose to share or send them, the app hands the finished file to an app you pick (WhatsApp, email, etc.), which then operates under that app’s own policies.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • On device: uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the local copy of your records.
  • On Drive: disable Drive backup and remove the app-data files from your Google account to delete the uploaded copies.
  • The developer does not host, store, or retain any copy of your records.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for use by adults in a professional railway S&T context and is not directed at children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
