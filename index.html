S&T Field Logbook

An offline field logbook for railway Signals & Telecommunications (S&T) department staff. Current version 1.7.8.17.

What it does

S&T Field Logbook helps S&T staff keep their day-to-day records in one place, entirely on the device:

  • Daily logs — station, Rest, Leave, CR, NH and Footplate; extra stops and temporary stations; work done, TA claim, tags, photos and PDFs.
  • Reports — Monthly, Diary, TA Journal, PCDO, Inspections, Tomorrow’s Work, and Muster (attendance sheet, 11th–10th). PDF, Word and Excel; colour or plain. Auto-generated timings.
  • Materials & notes — station stock, notes, and attachments.
  • Reminders — pending work, deficiencies, inspections and tag intervals.

Your data stays with you

Everything you enter is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. No account, analytics, advertising or tracking is required for normal use.

Optional Google Drive backup can be switched on in Settings. Saving and the first open of the day only back up days you changed. Two-way sync runs when you Sign in or tap Sync to Drive. The app uses only the private app-data folder in your own Google account, so your records can be restored after a reinstall. It never sees your other Drive files.
