S&T Field Logbook

An offline field logbook for railway Signals & Telecommunications (S&T) department staff.

What it does

S&T Field Logbook helps S&T staff keep their day-to-day records in one place, entirely on the device:

  • Daily logs — record the day’s work, notes and events.
  • Equipment records — track S&T equipment, their condition and maintenance.
  • Materials — keep a running record of materials and stock.
  • Timings — log and manage timings for signalling and interlocking work.
  • Reports — generate Word, Excel and PDF documents from your records and share them with whichever app you choose (WhatsApp, email, etc.).

Your data stays with you

Everything you enter is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. No account, analytics, advertising or tracking is required for normal use.

Optional Google Drive backup can be switched on in Settings. It uses only the private app-data folder in your own Google account, so your records can be restored after a reinstall or on a new device. The app never sees your other Drive files.
